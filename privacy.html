Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

This policy explains how the Lazy Reader app for Android ("the app"), made by Prerak's App World ("we"), handles information.

Information we collect

We (Prerak's App World) do not collect any personal information. The app has no account system, no advertising and no crash reporting service of its own. We have no server that receives your documents or your use of the app.

Google ML Kit (photo and translation features)

The photo-reading and translation features use Google ML Kit, which runs on your phone. Your photos, documents and translated text are processed on the phone and are not uploaded.

When you use these features, ML Kit sends Google diagnostic and usage information: device information (manufacturer, model, Android version), app information (package name and version), per-installation identifiers that are not intended to identify you, performance metrics, input and output sizes, event types and error codes, and for translation the languages you chose. Google uses this for diagnostics and usage analytics. This data is encrypted in transit. You can avoid it by not using the photo and translation features. See Google's description: developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

The first time you translate into a language, the app downloads that language pack (about 30 MB) from Google.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether a purchase has been made on your Google account.

Information stored on your phone

The documents you import, the text taken from them, your reading position, saved moments, notes and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are deleted when you remove a document in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include the app's data in your phone backup in your Google account. This is controlled by your phone's settings, not by us.

Speech (text to speech)

The app reads text aloud using your phone's text-to-speech engine, for example "Speech Services by Google". With the standard offline voices, speech is produced on your phone.

If you switch on Extra-natural online voices in Settings, the text being read is sent to the voice provider (for example Google) to produce speech. That processing is covered by the provider's own privacy policy. This option is off by default.

Web pages

When you share or paste a web link, the app downloads that one page directly from the website so it can be read aloud. The website receives a normal request from your phone, including your IP address, as with any browser. The app does not send cookies or log-in details, does not run scripts from the page, and keeps only the article text on your phone.

Clipboard

The app reads your clipboard only when you tap "Read what you copied" or open "Paste text / link". It does not read the clipboard in the background.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show playback controls while the app reads in the background.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: so reading continues when the screen is off.
  • Internet: for web links, optional online voices, Google Play purchases, translation language packs and ML Kit diagnostics.
  • Google Play billing: to sell and restore optional in-app purchases.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control all app data on your phone and can delete it at any time. For data that Google receives through ML Kit or Google Play, Google's privacy policy applies. You keep all rights given to you by data protection law, such as the GDPR.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
