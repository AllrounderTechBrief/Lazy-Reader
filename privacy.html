Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026

This policy covers Lazy Reader on every platform: the Android app, the Windows app (sold on the Microsoft Store as “LazyReader ReadAloud”), the web app at lazyreader.thestreamic.in/app, and this website. It is written to meet the EU and UK GDPR, the Irish Data Protection Act 2018, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, and US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act.

Who we are

Lazy Reader is made by Prerak's App World, Adamstown, Lucan, Dublin, Ireland (email thestreamic@gmail.com). On the Microsoft Store the app is published under the publisher name VistaOne. For the small amount of personal data described in this policy, Prerak's App World is the data controller (the “data fiduciary” under Indian law).

What we collect

We do not collect personal data through the apps or this website. The apps have no account system, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting of their own, and they never upload your documents to us.

If you email us, we receive your email address, name (if included) and your message. We use it only to reply and help you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). We keep the conversation for up to 24 months after it ends, then delete it. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail).

Cookies and browser storage

We do not use cookies. The web app keeps your documents, bookmarks, highlights and progress in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB) and your settings in local storage, only so the app works. This storage stays in your browser; we cannot see it. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it. Because it is strictly necessary for the service you asked for, no consent banner is needed.

How each app handles your information

Windows app (LazyReader ReadAloud)

  • Stored on your PC only: your library, the text of imported documents, bookmarks, highlights, notes, progress, settings and the app's own error logs, in your user folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\LazyReader, or the app's private folder when installed from the Microsoft Store). Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version removes them; if you used the downloaded installer, delete that folder to remove them.
  • Speech is made on your PC by the Kokoro AI voice model included with the app. Nothing you read is sent anywhere to produce speech.
  • No internet is used while you read. The app only goes online if you choose to: “Rate it” opens the Microsoft Store app. An optional local-AI download is switched off in this version; if it returns, it will ask you first, then download files from Hugging Face and GitHub, which see your IP address like any download.
  • Microsoft Store: Microsoft handles purchase, installation and updates under its own privacy statement. If Windows diagnostic data is turned on in your Windows settings, Microsoft may share crash and usage reports about the app with us as aggregated statistics. These reports do not include your documents.
  • Audio you export is saved where you choose and is labelled as AI-generated in its file details.

Web app

  • Files you open are read inside your browser and stored in your browser on this device. They are never uploaded.
  • Voices: the web app uses the voices your browser provides. Most are produced on your device, but some browsers offer online voices (for example Microsoft Edge “Online (Natural)” voices or Google voices in Chrome). If you pick one, your browser sends the text being read to that company to produce the speech, under its privacy policy. Offline voices keep the text on your device.
  • The app saves its own files in your browser so it can work offline. Fonts are included with the app, so no requests go to font services.

Android app

  • Documents, progress, bookmarks, notes and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. If Android device backup is on, Android may include this data in your backup in your Google account; this is controlled by your phone's settings.
  • Speech uses your phone's text-to-speech engine. Standard voices work on the phone. If you switch on Extra-natural online voices (off by default), the text being read is sent to the voice provider, for example Google.
  • Web links: when you share or paste a link, the app downloads that page directly from the website, which sees your IP address as with any browser. No cookies or log-ins are sent, and only the article text is kept.
  • Google ML Kit (photo reading and translation): your photos and text are processed on the phone and are not uploaded. When you use these features, ML Kit sends Google diagnostic data: device and app information, per-installation identifiers not intended to identify you, performance metrics, error codes and the chosen languages. The first translation into a language downloads that language pack from Google. See developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.
  • Purchases, if offered, are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details; the app only checks whether a purchase exists on your Google account.
  • The clipboard is read only when you tap “Read what you copied” or open “Paste text / link”.
  • Permissions: notifications and a foreground service (playback controls and reading with the screen off), and internet (web links, optional online voices, purchases, language packs and ML Kit diagnostics).

This website

No cookies, analytics or trackers. Fonts and images are served from this site itself. The site and web app are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web host, GitHub automatically records technical data about each visit, such as your IP address and browser, to deliver and protect the service, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Links to the Microsoft Store or Google Play take you to those companies' sites.

Other companies and international transfers

Google, Microsoft and GitHub act under their own privacy policies when you use their services described above. They may process data in the United States. These transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses used by those companies. We do not send them any personal data ourselves.

Your rights

Because your documents and reading data stay on your device, you control them directly: delete a document in the app, clear the app's or browser's data, or uninstall.

EU, UK and Ireland (GDPR): you can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of personal data we hold about you (in practice, only emails you sent us), restrict or object to its use, or ask for a copy. You can also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie) or the authority where you live.

India (DPDP Act 2023): you can ask for a summary of your personal data, its correction or erasure, and nominate another person to exercise your rights. Grievance contact: thestreamic@gmail.com, subject “Lazy Reader – grievance”. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

United States (including California): we do not sell or share personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or collect sensitive personal information. You can ask to know, correct or delete personal information we hold, and we will not treat you differently for doing so.

Children: Lazy Reader is a general reading tool, not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal data. Parents who believe a child has emailed us can ask us to delete it.

How to ask: email thestreamic@gmail.com with “Lazy Reader privacy” in the subject. We answer within one month (30 days for India). We may ask you to confirm the email address you used.

Automated decisions: we make no decisions about you by automated means and do no profiling.

Security: your data is protected by your device's own security. Keep your device locked and up to date.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date, and mention important changes in the app's release notes.
